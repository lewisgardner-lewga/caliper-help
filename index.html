Caliper theme documentation

Caliper help

Caliper is a Shopify theme for stores that sell by specification: parts, fixings, tools and supplies. Buyers search by part number, filter by size and rating, compare items side by side and order a whole list in one go.

Getting started

  1. Add Caliper from the Shopify Theme Store and choose a preset (Caliper, Hi-vis or Field).
  2. Before changing code, duplicate the theme in Online Store > Themes so you can always go back.
  3. Open Customize. In Theme settings > Logo and favicon, upload your logo and favicon. Without a logo, Caliper shows your store name.
  4. Set your colors in Theme settings > Colors and fonts in Typography.
  5. Add your product specifications (next sections). This is what makes Caliper's tables, filters and compare work.

Presets

The three presets share every feature and differ in colors, type and style settings. Switch presets in Theme settings; your content stays. The style guide shows every font, color scheme and button in each preset.

Spec data

Caliper reads specifications from product metafields. Create them once in Settings > Custom data > Products, then fill them per product or by CSV import.

1. Create spec fields

Make one single-line text metafield per specification, all in the specs namespace. For example specs.thread, specs.pressure, specs.material. Tick Storefronts access so the theme can read them.

2. Choose the key specs

In Theme settings > Product catalog > Key specifications, list the specs to show on product cards, the product readout and the compare table. One per line, as a label, a bar and the metafield:

Thread | specs.thread
Pressure | specs.pressure
Material | specs.material

A product can override this with its own list in a multi-line metafield caliper.key_specs, in the same format. Use this when one store sells several product types.

3. Spec columns per collection

Add a multi-line collection metafield caliper.spec_columns in the same format to set the table columns for that collection. A fittings collection can show thread and pressure while a bearings collection shows bore and outside diameter.

4. Full datasheet

The product page datasheet lists every line in caliper.spec_sheet, or the key specs if it is empty.

5. Documents and fitment

Sections with no data stay hidden, so you can add documents and fitment for some products and not others.

Collections and filters

Collection pages show products as a spec table or as cards. Buyers switch between them and Caliper remembers their choice. In the Collection catalog section you can set the default view, the number of spec columns, products per page, and whether to show the description and collection image.

Filters

Filters come from Shopify's free Search & Discovery app. Open the app, choose Filters and add availability, price, vendor, product type, variant options and any of your specs metafields. Caliper shows them as pills or as a checklist (Theme settings > Style > Filter style). On screens narrower than 1200px they open from a Filters button.

Stock

Stock counts come from Shopify inventory. Set Low stock threshold in Theme settings > Product catalog. Turn off Show stock status to hide counts.

Product page

Each part of the product page is a block you can reorder or remove in the Product datasheet section:

  • Title with part number, vendor and barcode
  • Spec readout: the first three key specs in large type
  • Price, and Price breaks for quantity pricing set up in B2B catalogs
  • Stock with an optional lead-time line, for example "Order by 3pm for same-day dispatch"
  • Variant picker, Buy buttons (with dynamic checkout and Shop Pay Installments) and Pickup availability
  • Description, Collapsible tabs, Text, Custom Liquid, Complementary products and app blocks
  • Specifications, Documents and Compatibility, laid out as an index or as cards (Theme settings > Style > Datasheet layout)

Compare

  1. Create a page called Compare in Online Store > Pages and choose the compare template.
  2. In Theme settings > Product catalog, turn on Enable compare and select the page as Compare page.

Buyers tick Compare on up to four products. A tray at the bottom of the screen keeps the selection and links to the compare page, which lines up the products' key specs and can show differences only.

Order by part number

Add the Order by part number section to any page, or create a page with the quick-order template. Buyers paste one part number and quantity per line, check the matches with stock and price, and add them all to the cart. Matching uses the variant SKU, so make sure your SKUs are filled in.

On the cart page, Copy as part list exports the cart in the same format for reordering.

Questions

My products show dashes in the spec table

The table columns come from the collection's caliper.spec_columns or the theme's key specifications. A dash means that product has no value for that metafield.

Filters do not appear

Add them in the Search & Discovery app. Metafield filters need the metafield definition to exist and at least one product to have a value.

Order by part number says "Not found"

Matching uses variant SKUs. Check the SKU is filled in and that the product is published to the Online Store.

Price breaks do not show

Quantity price breaks come from B2B catalogs, available on Shopify Plus. They show to customers logged in to a company account.

Can I use Caliper on more than one store?

Each theme purchase covers one store. Buy another copy for each additional store.

Support policy

We answer questions about the theme's settings and features, and we fix bugs in the theme. We reply within two business days, usually sooner.

  • Included: setup questions, how features work, and bugs in Caliper's own code.
  • Not included: custom code changes, third-party apps, and problems caused by edits to the theme's code. For custom work, hire a Shopify Partner.
  • Before editing code, duplicate your theme so you can go back.

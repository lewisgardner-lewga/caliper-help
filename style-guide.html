Spacing

Every section uses one spacing scale. Set a section's top and bottom padding in its settings; the theme scales it down on smaller screens so pages keep the same rhythm.

ScreenSection paddingHeading to contentGap between panels
Desktop, 990px and wider100% of the setting32px12px
Tablet, 600 to 989px80%24px12px
Phone, under 600px60%20px10px

Page margins follow Theme settings > Layout > Page margin and are capped at 20px on phones.
